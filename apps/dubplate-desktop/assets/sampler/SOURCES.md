# House sampler sounds

The six sounds use recordings from Versilian Studios LLC’s Versilian Community Sample Library. The upstream recordings are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The files bundled in Dubplate are edited at 48 kHz and stored as lossless FLAC. [Library](https://versilian-studios.com/vcsl/) · [Source license](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/blob/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/LICENSE)

- **Low tumba**. Low open hit, folded to mono with a short tail fade. Source audio — `Tumba_HitN_v3_rr1_Sum.wav`. [Source recordings](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/Membranophones/Struck%20Membranophones/Conga)
- **Muted bongo**. Right microphone, trimmed to the attack. Source audio — `BongoH_HitMuted1_v3_rr2_Mid.wav`. [Source recordings](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/Membranophones/Struck%20Membranophones/Bongos)
- **Low agogo**. Low bell with the room rumble filtered and the decay shortened to 410 ms. Source audio — `Agogo_Low_v2_rr1_Mid.wav`. [Source recordings](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/Idiophones/Struck%20Idiophones/Agogo%20Bells)
- **Room clap**. Three different claps spread over 17 ms with the low end filtered. Source audio — `Clap_rr1.wav`, `Clap_rr3.wav`, `Clap_rr5.wav`. [Source recordings](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/Idiophones/Struck%20Idiophones/Claps)
- **Shaker groove**. Eight recorded strokes arranged as a one-bar swung loop at 120 BPM. Source audio — `Mid_Shaker{High,Low}Faster_{Up,Down}_rr{1,2}.wav`. [Source recordings](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/Idiophones/Struck%20Idiophones/Shaker%2C%20Small)
- **Cm7 chord stab**. Yamaha TX81Z piano recordings voiced C3–G3–B♭3–E♭4, with soft saturation, a short envelope and stereo reflections. Source audio — `FMPiano_C3_vl2.wav`, `FMPiano_G#3_vl2.wav`, `FMPiano_C4_vl2.wav`, `FMPiano_E4_vl2.wav`. [Source recordings](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL/tree/c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e/Electrophones/TX81Z/FM%20Piano)
