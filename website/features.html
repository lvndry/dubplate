Find the next record.

Add music folders and browse their subfolders, or import your rekordbox collection with playlists, cues and beat grids. Copy a rekordbox playlist into Dubplate to make an editable version.

Build your own playlists, group them in folders, and rate, colour or tag tracks. Select a music folder to scope your search to it and the folders inside it.

Put beat one in place.

Dubplate analyses tracks in the background for energy on a scale of 1 to 10. For tracks without rekordbox analysis, it also detects BPM, key and the beat grid.

If an amapiano track’s first downbeat lands one beat late, move it back a beat. If the grid lines sit just behind the kicks, nudge them earlier in 5 ms steps and confirm the grid. The fix follows the track into sync and export.

Hear the handoff.

Build a running order and keep alternatives as backups. The Next panel suggests tracks after the one you select and shows how the key, tempo and energy would change.

Mark the mix-in and mix-out cues with I and O, then press S for a 20-second preview in your headphones. Mark a transition Free to leave it open for the room and turn off warnings for that handoff.

Read about transition preview ↗

Check the set.

Dubplate flags missing files, unconfirmed beat grids, and missing mix-in or mix-out cues. When a track has phrase data, Dubplate can propose cues for you to review.

Look for warnings between tracks too. If two vocals are about to overlap, try a different cue or use stems to mute the outgoing vocal.

Listen back to your mix.

Click REC or press ⌘⇧R to capture the master output as a 24-bit WAV. Dubplate saves it in Music/Dubplate Recordings.

Record a practice run and listen back to the bass swap on the way into the peak track. To share what you played, export a timestamped TXT or CSV tracklist from your Dubplate session history.

Take it to the booth.

Export a set, playlist or music folder with USB… for players that read classic Device Library, such as a CDJ-3000 or XDJ-RX3. The export copies the audio, track order, grids, cues, loops and waveforms. Set backups go in their own playlist.

Use a fresh stick or one Dubplate previously exported. For a player that requires OneLibrary, export rekordbox XML from Dubplate, import it into rekordbox 7, and export the USB there. Direct export refuses sticks that contain OneLibrary.

After playing, use Import from USB… in Library settings to read a classic Device Library stick. Bring back the CDJ’s track order and choose which cue changes to keep. The imported history contains track order only, and the stick stays read-only.
